Maintenance Mode

← Automation

A device enters maintenance only when the production traffic through it is proven quiesced, or can be moved away losslessly by redundancy that already exists in this network. Missing redundancy alone never blocks; active traffic with no proven lossless drain always does. The requested duration is the operator's intent and ends nothing; every maintenance has a hard safety deadline of {{ cycle_days }} days after entry, at which the worker starts the verified restore.
