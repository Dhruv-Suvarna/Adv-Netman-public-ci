Adopt an existing device

Adoption records an inventory.yml device that NMAS already writes to as a LEGACY_ADOPTION, IN_SERVICE asset. Its identity stays UNVERIFIED and no ZTP or commissioning history is created. Read-only devices (R5), hosts and the WebServer are refused. Repeating it changes nothing.
