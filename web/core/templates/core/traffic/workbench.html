What each test measures

ICMP pingReachability, loss and round-trip time. Not a throughput test.
Traceroute / pathThe hops towards the destination. A hop that does not answer is shown as silent.
TCP connectivityOne connection attempt to one port: connected, refused or timed out.
UDP probeA few datagrams to one port: refused (ICMP unreachable), or open-or-filtered. UDP cannot tell those two apart.
iperf3 throughputMeasured throughput over TCP or UDP (UDP adds jitter and loss). A temporary one-off server is started on the destination and removed afterwards.

Limits are enforced by the server, whatever a browser sends. The bitrate is always capped; there is no unlimited mode.
